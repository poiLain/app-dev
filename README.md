## 🎬 Movie Overview

**500 Days of Summer** is a 2009 romantic comedy-drama film directed by *Marc Webb*, starring **Joseph Gordon-Levitt** as Tom Hansen and **Zooey Deschanel** as Summer Finn.

The movie follows Tom, a young man who believes in true love and soulmates, as he falls in love with Summer, a woman who does not believe in traditional romantic relationships. Through a nonlinear timeline, the film explores their relationship over the course of 500 days, showing its happiest moments, misunderstandings, heartbreaks, and eventual separation.
